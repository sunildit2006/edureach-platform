# EduReach College Information

## About the College

EduReach College is an engineering college focused on technical education, innovation, and career development.

## Admissions

Students can apply for undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs.

## Courses

### Undergraduate Programs
- Computer Science and Engineering
- Information Technology
- Electronics and Communication Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Civil Engineering

### Postgraduate Programs
- M.Tech
- MBA
- MCA

## Campus Facilities

- Digital classrooms
- Computer laboratories
- Library
- Seminar halls
- Sports facilities
- Student activity centers

## Placement

The placement cell helps students prepare for technical interviews, aptitude tests, coding assessments, and recruitment drives.

## Student Support

Students can contact the college administration for information about admissions, courses, fees, examinations, placements, and campus facilities.
